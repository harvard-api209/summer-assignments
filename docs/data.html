Data dictionary

A browsable reference for data/development_indicators_2026.csv. Use it to pick indicators for your figures and the Part 5 memo without opening Posit Cloud. The same information lives in data/development_indicators_dictionary_2026.csv inside the project.

Unit of analysis

Each row is one country or economy in one year. These identifier columns tell you which row you are looking at:

Column What it is
country Country or economy name.
iso3c Three-letter country code, useful for joins and labels.
region World Bank region grouping.
income_group World Bank income classification (low, lower-middle, upper-middle, high).
is_lmic TRUE/FALSE: is this a low- or middle-income country?
assignment_sample TRUE/FALSE: flag for the sample used in some assignment questions.
year Calendar year of the observation.

Indicators

Most indicators come from the World Bank World Development Indicators (WDI). The last two come from the Worldwide Governance Indicators (WGI). "Higher means" tells you which direction a larger value points, so you do not misread a chart.

Variable Unit Higher means Notes
populationPopulation, total · WDI SP.POP.TOTL people larger population Total population.
gdp_per_capita_pppGDP per capita, PPP · WDI NY.GDP.PCAP.PP.KD constant international dollars higher income GDP per capita based on purchasing power parity.
gdp_growthGDP growth · WDI NY.GDP.MKTP.KD.ZG annual percent faster growth Annual growth rate of GDP at market prices, constant local currency.
inflationInflation · WDI FP.CPI.TOTL.ZG annual percent higher inflation Inflation measured by the consumer price index.
poverty_ratePoverty headcount at $3.65/day · WDI SI.POV.LMIC percent of population higher poverty Depends on household surveys, so coverage is sparse. Expect missing values.
giniGini index · WDI SI.POV.GINI index, 0–100 higher inequality Depends on household surveys, so coverage is sparse. Expect missing values.
life_expectancyLife expectancy at birth · WDI SP.DYN.LE00.IN years longer life expectancy Years a newborn would live if current mortality patterns stayed constant.
under5_mortalityUnder-5 mortality rate · WDI SH.DYN.MORT deaths per 1,000 live births worse child mortality Probability of dying between birth and age 5 per 1,000 live births.
secondary_school_enrollmentSecondary enrollment · WDI SE.SEC.ENRR gross enrollment percent higher enrollment Gross ratio can exceed 100 because of over-age or under-age students.
unemployment_totalUnemployment, total · WDI SL.UEM.TOTL.ZS percent of labor force higher unemployment Modeled ILO estimate.
unemployment_youthYouth unemployment · WDI SL.UEM.1524.ZS percent of labor force ages 15–24 higher youth unemployment Modeled ILO estimate.
co2_per_capitaCO2 emissions per capita · WDI EN.GHG.CO2.PC.CE.AR5 tons of CO2 equivalent per person higher emissions Excludes land use, land-use change, and forestry.
government_effectivenessGovernment effectiveness · WGI GE.EST estimate, about −2.5 to 2.5 more effective governance Perception-based composite indicator; compare changes cautiously.
control_of_corruptionControl of corruption · WGI CC.EST estimate, about −2.5 to 2.5 more control of corruption Perception-based composite indicator; compare changes cautiously.
Missing values are information: survey-based indicators like poverty_rate and gini are measured less often than vital statistics like life_expectancy. The assignments ask you to notice and explain missingness, not ignore it.

Use it in your work

In the assignments, read the data with the original path ../data/development_indicators_2026.csv and check this dictionary before interpreting any variable.
